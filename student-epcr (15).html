    Patient

    Never enter a real patient's name or identifying information.

    Identify the patient only as "Pt A," "Pt B," and so on. Do not use names, initials, dates of birth, addresses, phone numbers, medical record numbers, or photos, and do not name the patient anywhere in the narrative.

    Patient privacy is protected by federal law (HIPAA). Charts you submit here leave the clinical site and are seen by instructors, so putting identifying information in them is a privacy breach. It can result in removal from your clinical site and from the program.
