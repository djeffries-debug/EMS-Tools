Clinical performance policy

Students must actively seek to maximize their clinical experience by performing as many of the required skills as possible on live patients during field ride-alongs and hospital clinical rotations.

If a student does not get the opportunity to perform these skills on live patients because of call volume or patient presentations, the requirements may be simulated in class under the direct guidance and evaluation of a certified instructor to verify competency.

How signing works

Fill in an entry, then hand your device to the preceptor or instructor. They tap Get signature, review the entry, and sign with a finger or mouse. A signed entry locks so it can't be changed afterward. Only signed entries count toward requirements, and for skills with a Pass/Fail status only signed passes count.

Never enter patient names or identifying information. Record only the skill, clinical details, and vitals. No names, initials, dates of birth, addresses, or record numbers anywhere in this portfolio.
